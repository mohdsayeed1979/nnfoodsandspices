How we collect, use, and protect your information

1Information We Collect

We collect the minimum information needed to operate the App and fulfil your orders:

  • Account information — name, email address, and phone number when you register or sign in.
  • Delivery addresses — saved shipping addresses for order fulfilment.
  • Order history — products purchased, order status, and payment method selected (we do not store full card numbers — payments are handled by third-party payment processors, see Third-Party Services).
  • Wishlist and cart contents — stored on your device and, where you are signed in, associated with your account.
  • Search history — recent searches, stored locally on your device to improve your experience.
  • Device and usage information — app version, device model, and crash diagnostics (see Firebase Usage).

2Device Permissions

The App requests the following permissions, only for the features that need them:

PermissionUsed for
CameraScanning product barcodes and attaching photos (e.g. to reviews or support requests)
MicrophoneVoice search
NotificationsOrder updates, offers, and promotions
Internet / Network StateLoading the product catalog, processing orders, and syncing your account

You can deny or revoke any of these permissions at any time from your device settings; the App will continue to work, with the corresponding feature (e.g. barcode scanning) simply unavailable.

3Images & Camera

Photos you choose to capture or select (for example, when scanning a barcode or attaching an image to a support request) are processed on your device. We do not access your photo library or camera in the background, and we do not collect images without an explicit action from you.

4Notifications

The App can send you local and push notifications about order status changes, offers, and promotions. Push notifications (when enabled by our backend) are delivered via Firebase Cloud Messaging. You can disable notifications at any time from the App's Settings screen or your device's notification settings.

5Internet Usage

The App requires an internet connection to load the product catalog, process orders, and sync your account across devices. All network communication is encrypted using HTTPS. Core browsing and cart functionality remain available with cached data when offline.

6Firebase Usage

The App is built to use Google Firebase for the following (where enabled on our backend):

  • Firebase Cloud Messaging — delivering push notifications.
  • Firebase Analytics — understanding aggregate app usage to improve features (see Analytics).
  • Firebase Crashlytics — collecting crash reports to help us fix bugs.

Firebase's own privacy practices are described at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

7Analytics

We use aggregate, anonymised analytics (via Firebase Analytics) to understand which features are used most, identify performance issues, and guide future improvements. This data is not used to individually identify you for advertising purposes.

8Third-Party Services

The App is designed to work with the following categories of third-party services:

  • Payment processors (e.g. Razorpay, Stripe, PayTabs, Google Pay, Apple Pay) — process your payment details directly; we do not store your full card or bank details on our servers.
  • Firebase / Google Cloud — notifications, analytics, and crash reporting as described above.
  • Delivery & logistics partners — receive the delivery address and contact details necessary to fulfil your order.

Each third-party service processes data under its own privacy policy in addition to this one.

9Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only:

  • With service providers strictly as needed to operate the App (payment processing, delivery, cloud infrastructure);
  • To comply with a legal obligation, court order, or governmental request; or
  • To protect the rights, property, or safety of Nujju's Nest Spices Pvt. Ltd., our users, or the public.

10Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including:

  • All network traffic encrypted in transit via HTTPS.
  • Passwords are never stored in plain text.
  • Session tokens are stored using platform-level encrypted storage (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain).

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; we work to protect your information but cannot guarantee absolute security.

11Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the relevant minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.
